About the guide

Local Hits finds places with a Wikipedia story within a five-kilometre radius. Toronto opens with an edited selection. Searching an area adds up to 60 nearby English Wikipedia pages, ordered by straight-line distance.

Food, Nightlife and Sights are quick filters. Wikipedia coverage varies between neighbourhoods. Venue histories can outlast businesses, so check opening hours before a visit.

Place names go to Photon, using OpenStreetMap data. If you choose “Use my location”, coordinates go to Photon and Wikipedia to find nearby places.

Article introductions are excerpted from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each place links to its article and contributor history.
