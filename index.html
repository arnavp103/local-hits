    About the guide

    Local Hits finds up to 60 nearby English Wikipedia pages and adds edited notes where available. Walk times use straight-line distance at 4.8 km/h with a 25% allowance for street layout. The 30-minute cutoff is an estimate. Bridges, hills and crossings can change the actual route.

    Food, Nightlife and Sights are quick filters. Wikipedia coverage varies between neighbourhoods. Venue histories can outlast businesses, so check opening hours before a visit.

    As you type, search suggestions come from Photon, using OpenStreetMap data. If you choose “Use my location”, coordinates go to Photon and Wikipedia to find nearby places.

    Article introductions are excerpted from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each place links to its article and contributor history.
